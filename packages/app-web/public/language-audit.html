Property names: one word, several meanings

termspreadwhat it covers
kind2 meaningsClaim.kind is one of exploratory or confirmatory. Artefact.kind is a free string. The same word, no shared values, and resolution_kind on a Decision is a third list.
subject3 thingsAn act’s subject is what the command addressed or created. An event’s dir: subject means the change is to the record itself, whatever the act was about. The subject link on an act points at the first, and acts:about on a record collects the second.
event2 thingsDomainEvent and the domain_event table hold one act each, with its changes inside. /{id}/events lists the changes that name a record, so an event there is one change, not one act.
outputs / mayRead2 shapesBoth plural, both on Task. mayRead is a list and outputs is one string.

Method

Terms are lowercased, split on camelCase and underscores, stemmed, and stripped of stopwords. CLI = every .command() name. Domain = NODE_LABELS + EDGE_LABELS. Scenarios = the words of each describe() title. Stemming merges gate/gates and evaluate/evaluated, which is why the overlap is generous rather than strict. Property names = the fields of each *Props interface, checked against the API’s responses across three workspaces.
