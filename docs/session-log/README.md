# Session log — closed 2026-08-29

**Nothing writes here any more.** Seventy-two entries, 18–29 August. They stay
exactly as written: they are dated records, so they cannot go stale and must not
be corrected. Read them for what a session did on the day it says.

**What replaced them is the pull request body**, which carries the same five
things — the goal, what changed, the commands that verified it with their real
output, what was left open, the next step — while being durable, addressable,
attached to the diff it describes, and free. The entries had become a second
copy of the PR that produced them.

**What ended it was the cost, measured rather than felt.** 174 of this
repository's 528 commits since 18 August touched nothing but this directory: a
third of the history was the write-up of the other two thirds. The argument is
in PR #117, along with what the hook was genuinely catching and what replaced
that.

**A belief that will be revised belongs in neither.** Contested claims and the
reason one was overturned live in the exo-ledger store, which can hold two
positions open until evidence settles them. A markdown file cannot, and trying
is what made this directory grow.

Below is what the entries were, while they were being written.

---

Mechanical wrap-ups, one per working session, written by the `wrap` skill
(`.claude/skills/wrap/`, deleted with this change) and usually triggered by its
Stop hook rather than by anyone remembering. Each entry records the goal in a
line, what changed, the commands that verified it with their real output, what
was left open, and the exact next step.

**This is not `docs/project-journal/`.** That sequence is a curated argument —
each entry is a decision and the reasoning behind it, and CLAUDE.md points
readers at the newest few for what is true now. These are a handover for the
next session, and are disposable in a way the journal is not. Numbering here is
independent and starts at `001`.

If a wrap turns out to contain a real decision, that decision belongs in the
journal as well, in the journal's own terms — not by reference to a file here.
